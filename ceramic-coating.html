What a Ceramic Coating Does

A ceramic coating bonds to your paint and forms a hard, glossy, clear layer on top of it. Dirt, dust and water have a harder time sticking, so washes go faster and the paint holds its shine longer.

That layer also helps shield the paint from everyday wear, like sun, bird droppings and road grime, which matters when your car sits outside in the Imperial Valley heat.

What it is not: a coating is not scratch-proof, it will not fix paint that is already swirled or dull, and it does not mean you never wash your car again. The paint underneath decides how good the result looks, which is why preparation matters most.

How the Application Works

  1. 1. Deep clean and decontamination

    We start with a thorough hand wash, then use a clay bar to lift the embedded contaminants that regular washing cannot reach.

  2. 2. Paint correction, if needed

    A coating seals the paint exactly as it is, swirls included. If your paint needs it, we recommend correcting swirl marks and light scratches first so the coating goes over a smooth, clear surface.

  3. 3. Surface prep

    We wipe every panel down with isopropyl alcohol to strip away leftover residue, so the coating can bond properly.

  4. 4. Coating application

    We apply the ceramic coating one section at a time with microfiber applicators, then level it and let it cure into a hard, clear layer over the paint.

  5. 5. Final inspection and care tips

    We check the whole car under good light, walk it with you, and explain how to wash it and what to avoid while the coating cures.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a ceramic coating do?
It adds a clear protective layer on top of the paint. The surface becomes glossier and slicker, so dirt and water rinse off more easily.
How long does a ceramic coating last?
Several years is common, depending on the product, how the car is cared for and the conditions it lives in.
Will it fix scratches?
No. A coating seals in the paint exactly as it is, including swirls and scratches. If your paint needs it, we recommend paint correction first, so the coating goes over the best finish possible.
Can I still wash my car?
Yes, and you should. Regular washing keeps a coating working well. We show you how to wash it safely when we hand the car back.
