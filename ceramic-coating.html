Straight answers

What ceramic coating does and does not do

  • Adds a protective layer over the paint.
  • Makes dust, bugs and water easier to wash off.
  • Paint enhancement (4-year and up) improves gloss before coating.
  • It does not prevent every water spot.
  • It does not remove every scratch or defect.
  • It does not replace regular, careful washing.

Before Andre quotes

Ceramic and correction jobs need your vehicle, its paint condition and your goals. Andre may ask for photos, or look at the paint in person, before giving a final price.

Ceramic coating questions

Will it stop water spots from our hard water?
No coating prevents every water spot. A coating makes the surface easier to clean, so spots are easier to remove when you wash regularly.
Will it remove scratches?
The coating itself does not. The 4-year package includes paint enhancement, and the 7-year and 10-year packages include correction steps meant to reduce visible swirls and scratches. Not every defect can be removed.
Do you come to me?
Yes. ACleaner Detailer is mobile and serves Calexico and the Imperial Valley. Tell Andre your city when you ask for a quote.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes, with 48 hours' notice, including for weather. Refunds apply when you cancel with 48 hours' notice.
