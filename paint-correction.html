What Paint Correction Does

Most swirl marks and light scratches come from washing and drying, automatic car washes and everyday wear. They sit in the clear coat, the transparent top layer of your paint, and scatter light so the color looks flat and hazy.

Paint correction uses a machine polisher to remove a very thin layer of that clear coat and level the surface. Once it is smooth, light reflects evenly and the paint looks clearer, deeper and glossier.

It works on the clear coat only. If a scratch goes through to the color or primer, polishing can soften it but not remove it, and we tell you that up front.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is paint correction?
Paint correction is machine polishing that levels out fine marks in the clear coat, so light reflects evenly and the paint looks clearer and glossier.
What can it fix, and what can it not?
It reduces swirl marks, light scratches and dull, faded clear coat. Scratches that cut through the clear coat may only be softened, not removed.
Do I need it before a ceramic coating?
Often, yes. A coating locks in the paint exactly as it is, so any swirls stay sealed under it. Correcting first gives the coating the best surface to bond to. We can advise once we see the car.
Will the swirls come back?
They can if the paint is washed or dried roughly. Clean wash mitts and soft microfiber towels keep new swirls from forming, and a ceramic coating makes washing easier.
