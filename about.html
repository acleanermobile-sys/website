Service area

ACleaner Detailer is based in Calexico and serves the Imperial Valley, California. Tell Andre your city in the quote form and he'll confirm before booking.

Vehicles: cars, trucks, SUVs, large SUVs, motorcycles, RVs and off-road vehicles such as RZRs. Unusual vehicles are quoted after Andre confirms the details.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book?
Ask for a quote with the form, or call or text (760) 925-1862. Andre confirms the price and the service, then you pick a time.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes, with 48 hours' notice. Refunds apply when you cancel with 48 hours' notice.
What if the weather is bad?
Weather cancellations and rescheduling are allowed with 48 hours' notice.
What if I'm late or not there?
Late arrivals and no-shows are not refunded. Rescheduling may be allowed.
How can I pay?
Zelle is accepted. Ask about other options when you book.
Do I need to be home?
Andre needs access to the vehicle, and to the inside for interior work. Details are confirmed when you book.
Do add-ons cost extra?
Yes. Add-ons are discussed once the main service is confirmed, and nothing is added without your OK. See the services page.
